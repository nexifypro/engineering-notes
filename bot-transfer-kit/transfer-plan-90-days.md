# 90-day transfer plan

Starts the first month in which all scorecard gates are green or amber with named actions.
Each week has one checkpoint, chaired by the client engineering lead, with the vendor present.

## Weeks 1–2 · Inventory and intent
- Platform ownership matrix completed and signed by both sides.
- Named transferee for every system, every rota and every third-party relationship.
- People plan confirmed: who transfers, who stays with the vendor, who is hired; offers issued.
- Knowledge gaps listed from the scorecard; teach-back sessions scheduled.

## Weeks 3–4 · Shadow reversal
- Client engineers lead; vendor engineers shadow. Applies to deployments, incident response, on-call, hiring interviews.
- First account transfers: cloud organisation, identity provider, source control.
- Secrets rotation plan agreed; rotation starts with anything the vendor could read.

## Weeks 5–6 · Access and billing
- All billing accounts in the client's name.
- Vendor standing admin removed everywhere; break-glass procedure documented and tested once.
- Remaining account transfers: DNS, certificates, registries, app stores, observability, pager.

## Weeks 7–8 · Observer window
- Two weeks with vendor engineers in observer mode only.
- Delivery metrics compared with the agreed bands. Any breach becomes a named action.
- Runbook gaps found in the window fixed by the client team.

## Weeks 9–10 · Contracts and knowledge sign-off
- Vendor contracts novated or re-signed; support plans in the client's name.
- Architecture decisions, system map and data flows reviewed and signed off by client engineers.
- Hiring pipeline fully client-run.

## Weeks 11–12 · Transfer and retention
- Transfer certificate signed: scorecard all green, matrix complete, access review clean.
- Retention arrangement starts: named vendor contacts, hours cap, response times, end date.
- Thirty-day post-transfer review booked.

## Checkpoint agenda (weekly, 30 minutes)
1. Scorecard changes since last week.
2. Ownership matrix rows still open.
3. Risks to the date and the named owner for each.
4. Decisions needed from either side.
