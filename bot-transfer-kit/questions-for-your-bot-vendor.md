# Questions to ask any Build-Operate-Transfer vendor, including us

Ask these before signing. The answers tell you whether transfer is designed in or hoped for.

1. **What, exactly, is the transfer trigger?** A date, a set of measurable gates, or both? Who scores the gates and how often?
2. **Which gates do you propose, with thresholds?** If the answer is "stability" or "maturity" without a number, keep asking.
3. **What is your commercial incentive to transfer on time?** A holdback or success fee released at transfer is aligned. A day rate with no end is not.
4. **Who owns every account from day one?** Cloud organisation, identity provider, source control, DNS, app stores, billing. Ask for the ownership matrix before the kickoff.
5. **How do you avoid holding standing admin?** What is your break-glass procedure and how is it logged?
6. **What happens to the people?** Who is on your payroll, who is on ours, when offers are made, what the retention terms are, and what you do if someone declines to transfer.
7. **How do you run the observer window?** Two weeks with your engineers watching only, before transfer, with metrics compared to agreed bands.
8. **What knowledge transfer is acceptance-tested?** Teach-back sessions where our engineer performs the task, not a slide deck.
9. **What is the post-transfer arrangement?** Named contacts, hours cap, response times, end date, price.
10. **Show us a transfer you completed.** Dates, what went wrong, what you changed afterwards. A vendor who has never finished one is selling managed services.
